How it works, in four steps.

  1. 1

    We talk.

    We call you, you tell us about your restaurant and how you take bookings today. If it suits you, we send you a clear quote.

  2. 2

    You give us what you already have.

    Menu and prices, opening hours, photos, the story of the place, your tables and shifts. If you already have a website or a booking diary, we start from there.

  3. 3

    We prepare everything, you see it first.

    Website, diary with your tables and assistant: you try them in a preview with test bookings, and you approve them.

  4. 4

    It goes live.

    The website goes online, the form and the chat take bookings, and we stay close to you during your first services.

While you're busy in the dining room.

  • Bookings from your website's form and chat go straight into the diary, and you or your staff add phone and walk-in ones to the same diary. WhatsApp: coming soon.
  • The assistant answers guests on your website about opening hours, menu and bookings, using only information you have approved. It doesn't make things up: when it can't answer, the conversation comes to you.
  • Your website: we build it, and you change it by writing a message on Telegram or in the app. You see a preview, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

One subscription, together with your website.

Month by month
You pay for a first period, then it renews one month at a time.
The whole year
You pay for twelve months in one go, then it renews year by year.

We prepare the quote after talking to you: it sets out the figures and the terms. You pay only when you accept it, and the paid period starts when the service goes live.
